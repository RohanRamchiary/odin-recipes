Chicken Yakitori

Home

Description:

Chicken Yakitori is a popular Japanese dish made from bite-sized pieces of chicken grilled on skewers. The chicken is usually seasoned with a savory-sweet sauce called tare, made with soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar. The skewers are grilled over a hot flame, giving the chicken a delicious smoky flavor and lightly charred edges.

Yakitori can be made using different parts of the chicken, including thighs, breast, wings, and even chicken skin. It is commonly served as an appetizer or snack and is often enjoyed with rice, vegetables, or a dipping sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 500 g boneless chicken thighs

  • ½ cup soy sauce

  • ¼ cup mirin

  • 2 tablespoons sake

  • 2 tablespoons sugar

  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger

  • 2–3 green onions

  • Bamboo skewers

Steps:

  1. Prepare the chicken: Cut the boneless chicken thighs into small, bite-sized pieces.

  2. Make the sauce: In a bowl, mix together the soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, garlic, and grated ginger.

  3. Marinate the chicken: Add the chicken pieces to the sauce and let them marinate for at least 30 minutes.

  4. Prepare the skewers: Cut the green onions into small pieces. Thread the chicken and green onion pieces onto the bamboo skewers, alternating between them.

  5. Grill the yakitori: Preheat a grill or pan over medium-high heat. Place the skewers on the grill and cook for about 4–5 minutes on each side.

  6. Add the sauce: Brush the remaining sauce over the chicken while grilling. Turn the skewers occasionally until the chicken is fully cooked and lightly charred.

  7. Serve: Remove the skewers from the grill and serve the yakitori hot.
