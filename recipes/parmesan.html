Chicken Parmesan

Home

Description:

Chicken Parmesan is a classic Italian-American dish made with tender chicken breasts coated in seasoned breadcrumbs, fried or baked until golden and crispy, and topped with rich tomato sauce and melted cheese. The combination of crunchy chicken, flavorful marinara sauce, and gooey cheese makes it a comforting and satisfying meal.

Usually served with pasta, salad, or crusty bread, Chicken Parmesan is a popular choice for both casual dinners and special occasions. Its delicious balance of crispy, savory, and cheesy flavors makes it a favorite among people of all ages.

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts

  • 1 cup breadcrumbs

  • ½ cup all-purpose flour

  • 2 eggs

  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

  • 1 cup marinara sauce

  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

  • ½ teaspoon salt

  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

  • Fresh basil or parsley for garnish
