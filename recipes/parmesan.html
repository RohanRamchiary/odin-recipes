Chicken Parmesan

Home

Description:

Chicken Parmesan is a classic Italian-American dish made with tender chicken breasts coated in seasoned breadcrumbs, fried or baked until golden and crispy, and topped with rich tomato sauce and melted cheese. The combination of crunchy chicken, flavorful marinara sauce, and gooey cheese makes it a comforting and satisfying meal.

Usually served with pasta, salad, or crusty bread, Chicken Parmesan is a popular choice for both casual dinners and special occasions. Its delicious balance of crispy, savory, and cheesy flavors makes it a favorite among people of all ages.

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts

  • 1 cup breadcrumbs

  • ½ cup all-purpose flour

  • 2 eggs

  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

  • 1 cup marinara sauce

  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

  • ½ teaspoon salt

  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

  • Fresh basil or parsley for garnish

Steps:

  1. Prepare the chicken: Flatten the chicken breasts to an even thickness and season both sides with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.

  2. Set up the coating: Place flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan cheese into three separate bowls.

  3. Coat the chicken: Dip each chicken breast into the flour, then the beaten eggs, and finally the breadcrumb-Parmesan mixture. Make sure it is evenly coated.

  4. Cook the chicken: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the chicken for about 3–4 minutes on each side, until golden brown and nearly cooked through.

  5. Add the sauce: Transfer the chicken to a baking dish and spoon marinara sauce over each piece.

  6. Add the cheese: Sprinkle shredded mozzarella and a little Parmesan cheese over the top of each chicken breast.

  7. Bake: Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for about 15–20 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and the cheese is melted and bubbly.

  8. Serve: Garnish with fresh basil or parsley and serve hot with pasta, salad, or crusty bread.
