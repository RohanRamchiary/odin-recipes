Chicken Teriyaki

Home

Description:

Chicken Teriyaki is a popular Japanese-inspired dish made with tender pieces of chicken coated in a rich, glossy teriyaki sauce. The sauce combines soy sauce, sugar, ginger, and garlic to create a delicious balance of sweet, salty, and savory flavors. The chicken is usually pan-fried or grilled until golden and juicy, then coated with the sauce until it becomes thick and caramelized.

Often served over steamed white rice, Chicken Teriyaki makes a simple yet satisfying meal. The tender chicken, flavorful sauce, and fluffy rice come together to create a comforting dish that is both easy to prepare and full of flavor. It can also be garnished with sesame seeds and sliced green onions for extra texture and freshness.

Ingredients:

  • 500 g boneless chicken thighs or chicken breast

  • ½ cup soy sauce

  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar

  • 1 tablespoon honey

  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar

  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger

  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil

  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch

  • 2 tablespoons water

  • 2 green onions, sliced

  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds

  • Steamed white rice, for serving
